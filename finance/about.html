A personal project combining Google Gemini AI for automated expense categorization with real-time collaboration tools — giving individuals and groups complete clarity over their financial lives.

Core Features
AI Categorization
Google Gemini 1.5 Flash automatically classifies every transaction into the correct spending category in real time.
Smart Budgeting
AI reviews 3 months of spending history and generates a personalized, optimized monthly budget with actionable tips.
Group Collaboration
Create shared financial groups for households, roommates, or teams with role-based member access (Admin / Contributor / Viewer).
Visual Analytics
Dynamic Chart.js visualizations — bar charts, radar health scores, doughnuts, and trend lines — for deep financial insight.
